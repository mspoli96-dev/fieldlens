# FieldLens

FieldLens is an independent Webytex open-source demonstration of visual assistance for business support teams. All source, comments, interface copy, fixtures, documentation, and articles are in English. Converse with Martin in Spanish.

Only this project and its own review artifacts are in scope. Preserve other demos and client projects. Do not copy credentials, customer data, media, or client code. The fictional FL-01 desk printer and its procedure are original demo materials.

Starting another project requires his next explicit approval. Purchases, top-ups, and additional paid subscriptions require a concrete cost disclosure and approval.
